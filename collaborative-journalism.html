Collaborative Journalism

MEDIA will leverage its influence to join the continuing growth of collaboration in the media industry especially with a focus on partnerships that produce positive community impact. Combining social sciences and humanities traditions with practice-based, design-oriented interventions to understand collaborative journalism, MEDIA encourages experiments on joint newsrooms for local and regional newspapers to highlight the potential of pooled resources and expertise.
