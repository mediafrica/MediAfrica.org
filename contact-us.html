F.A.Qs

What is Media Education Development Initiatives (MEDIA)?

Media Education Development Initiatives is a platform dedicated to fostering media literacy, journalism education, policy advocacy, and support for struggling news organizations.

Who can benefit from the programs and initiatives?

Journalists, students, educators, media professionals, policymakers, and anyone interested in media literacy and responsible journalism can benefit from the various programs and resources.

How does the organization support struggling news organizations?

Support is provided through financial assistance, grants, and collaborative projects that help small, independent media outlets sustain operations in economically struggling regions.

What types of media literacy programs are available?

Programs include media literacy workshops, training for journalists, data journalism support, and the development of educational materials to counter misinformation.
