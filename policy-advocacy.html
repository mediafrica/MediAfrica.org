Policy & Advocacy

At Media Education Development Initiatives (MEDI), we believe that sustainable change begins with strong policies. Through evidence-based advocacy, public engagement, and strategic partnerships, we champion media and communication policies that empower communities, promote equity, and build resilience against disinformation.

Our Advocacy Focus Areas

1

Inclusive Media Literacy Policies

We advocate for national and local policies that prioritize media literacy for all—particularly vulnerable populations, including racial and ethnic minorities, low-income groups, and individuals with limited digital access or education.

2

Stronger Regulations for Local Journalism

We support regulatory frameworks that encourage media companies to invest in local journalism, especially in underserved and economically struggling communities.

3

Community-Led Initiatives

Through grassroots campaigns, volunteer programs, and partnerships, we empower citizens to become active participants in shaping their media environments. Our trained volunteers help deliver localized media literacy curricula to their communities.

What We Do?

1

Engage Policymakers & Stakeholders

We organize dialogues, workshops, and roundtables to engage decision-makers and highlight the role of media literacy in democracy and development.

2

Promote Equity in Digital Access

We push for policies that bridge the digital divide, ensuring marginalized groups gain access to critical tools and information.

3

Support Public Awareness Campaigns

We design and launch targeted campaigns that inform citizens about their media rights, digital responsibilities, and how to recognize disinformation.

4

Contribute to Global Policy Discourse

We collaborate with international bodies like the United Nations to contribute to policy development for the Global South, with a focus on academic access and social equity.
