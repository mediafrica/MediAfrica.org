IMT, Enugu gets News Lab soon

The Institute of Management and Technology, Enugu and MEDIA are developing a Local News Lab in the Department of Mass Communications.

Why it matters

The lab will strengthen local journalism while giving future media professionals a place to learn by doing. It is designed to broaden whose stories are told and who gets to participate in public life.

A newsroom, classroom, and public forum

Through MEDIA’s institutional and international partnerships, the lab will encourage joint faculty research, student reporting, and collaboration between higher education institutions in Nigeria and beyond.

Local reportingStories that fill gaps in community news.
ResearchAnnual insight into the state of local news.
Emerging leadersTraining, mentorship, podcasting, and public dialogue.

The lab will also convene experts and policymakers around technology, AI education, content creation, and the future of local news.
